# Unit Rate Showdown

Math 2201 (Unit 6, Sections 1 to 2, Proportional Reasoning) formative practice app. Indicators 6.16, 6.17, 6.22, 6.23.

Students enter the unit rate for each of two or three generated offers and pick the better buy, then handle constraint rounds where a required quantity forces whole packages or whole cases, so the cheapest unit rate can lose on total cost. A work-backwards mode gives a total and a rate and asks for the missing quantity, cost or time. Feedback is consequence-based on the constraint rounds (packs bought, totals, leftovers) and names likely errors elsewhere. A printable decision sheet compiles the offers, the student's unit rates, their pick and their typed justification for the assignment folder.

Fresh generated values on every round; nothing is a fixed bank. Single-file HTML, no external dependencies, no student data leaves the page.
