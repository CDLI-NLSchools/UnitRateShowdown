Practice comparing offers with unit rates, handling whole packs and whole cases, and working backwards from a total. This is formative practice: try, check, read the feedback, and try again. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves this page.

Unit Rate Rounds

Work out the unit rate for each offer (price divided by quantity), type it in, then pick the better buy: the offer with the lowest unit rate.

Which offer is the better buy?

Constraint Rounds: Whole Packs and Whole Cases

Sometimes you cannot buy exactly what you need. You must buy whole packs or whole cases, so compare total cost, not just the unit rate. The offer with the cheapest unit rate can lose once leftovers are paid for.

Which option costs the least in total?

Work Backwards

You are given a rate and one value. Isolate the missing variable: divide the total by the rate, or multiply the rate by the amount, whichever fits the units.
